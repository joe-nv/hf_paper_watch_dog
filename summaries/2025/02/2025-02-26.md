- **核心观点（观点）**: 本研究聚焦于**训练与强化学习**（Training & RL）赛道，属于算法与架构创新。针对当前领域面临的「现有方法在复杂场景下的性能与效率权衡」痛点，作者围绕 **Scaling LLM Pre-training with Vocabulary** 展开攻关，核心切入概念涵盖大语言模型（Large Language Model (LLM)）。核心论点主张：To bridge this gap, we introduce vocabulary curriculum learning, an approach that improves pretraining efficiency with log-linear scaling gains relative to vocabulary size。
